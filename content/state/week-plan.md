body:
Your agency gets a pay rise every time your cost per acquisition goes up.

Not deliberately. It's arithmetic. They charge a percentage of spend, so if your CPA doubles you need twice the budget for the same number of sales. Same outcome for you. Double the fee for them.

The incentive to make an account more efficient runs backwards. And it never looks like that from the inside, because more budget gets called scaling.

At $30,000 a month on twenty per cent, the fee is $72,000 a year. Double the spend and it's $144,000. Same work.

The fix isn't complicated. Price the work, not the budget. It just doesn't grow as nicely.
