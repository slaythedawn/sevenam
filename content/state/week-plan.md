### LinkedIn · the model · medium

audience: founders and marketing leads | feeling: recognition | job: explain
body:
Here's the bit that took me too long to see.

An agency on ten per cent of spend earns more when your cost per acquisition gets worse.

Not because anyone plans it that way. If your CPA doubles, you need twice the budget for the same number of sales. Same outcome for you. Double the fee for them.

So the incentive to make an account more efficient runs backwards. And it never looks like that from the inside, because more budget gets called scaling.

The fix isn't complicated. Price the work, not the budget. It just doesn't grow as nicely.
