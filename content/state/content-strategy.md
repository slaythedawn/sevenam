# Content strategy: the series, the modes, the rhythm

Written 29 Aug 2026 on Josh's instruction: develop content themes that repeat on a
schedule, across four modes. Read with `audience.md`, which says who this is for and where
they are going.

**The principle.** A feed works when a viewer starts to recognise what they are looking at
before they read it. That needs named series that recur, each with its own visual treatment,
not a stream of one-off posts. Eight series, four modes, one palette.

---

## The four modes, and the weekly split

Josh's framing, 29 Aug 2026.

| Mode | What it does | Share of the week |
|---|---|---|
| **Aspiration** | Sells the position they end up holding | ~2 in 7 |
| **Informative** | Proof from inside real accounts | ~3 in 7 |
| **Education** | The method, given away in full | ~2 in 7 |
| **Lifestyle** | Training and ordinary life | ~2 in 7 |

Overlaps because Instagram carries more lifestyle and LinkedIn more education. The mix
matters more than the arithmetic.

## The eight series

### 1. 7am — *informative*

The daily decision, written before anything else. The signature ritual and the only thing
here nobody else can post.

**Treatment.** Phone-filmed screen at dawn, volt redaction bars over every figure.
**Cadence.** Daily when the numbers exist. **Never invent one to fill the slot.**
**Channels.** All three.

### 2. Inside the Account — *informative*

The format the reference posts use, rebuilt so it is publishable. A phone filming a real
screen: the queue of creative, the account structure, a decision being made, the volume of
work going out.

**The rule that makes it possible.** Film the system, redact the money. **Volt bars over
every spend figure, ROAS, revenue number and client name.** No exceptions, no "it is only a
small number". The redaction is the design, and it says real account, numbers withheld,
which is both true and more interesting than the figure would have been.

**Treatment.** Filmed off the screen on a phone, never a clean screenshot. Slight angle,
moiré, a hand or finger in frame. Plain text overlay, black on white, two lines maximum.
**Cadence.** Two a week. **Channels.** Instagram and LinkedIn; a still to X.

### 3. The Teardown — *informative and education*

A public ad from the Meta Ad Library, dismantled. Free, infinite, unfakeable, and it touches
no client account at all.

**Treatment.** The ad, marked up with the volt annotation layer — circles, arrows, a rule
under the thing that matters. **Cadence.** One a week. **Channels.** LinkedIn and Instagram
carousel.

### 4. The Method — *education*

How the work is actually done, published in full. No withheld step, no gated framework.

**Treatment.** Design-led. Cards, a framework, a diagram. This is where the four design-led
posts a week live. **Cadence.** One a week. **Channels.** LinkedIn, Instagram carousel.

### 5. One Thing — *education*

A single mechanism, explained short. Why a metric lies, what frequency is telling you, what
leaves the learning phase.

**Treatment.** Statement card or a marked-up frame. **Cadence.** Two or three a week.
**Channels.** X primarily, it is the format X rewards.

### 6. The Position — *aspiration*

Where they end up. An account they own, margin that survives their own growth, a business
somebody else could run or buy. This is the aspiration mode and it is aimed at **their**
destination, never at what Josh owns.

**Treatment.** The aspirational photography — scale, standard, early light, real places.
Sydney is genuinely aspirational and genuinely available.
**Cadence.** Two a week. **Channels.** All three.

### 7. The Mission — *lifestyle*

Training. Black and white, obscured. Personal growth beside the work, never explained as a
metaphor for it.

**Treatment.** High contrast monochrome, **no volt at all**, so the grid separates the two
threads at a glance. **Cadence.** One or two a week, never consecutive days.
**Channels.** Instagram led.

### 8. Off Duty — *lifestyle*

Ordinary life. Family, the car, the walk, the coffee, the thing that went wrong. Warmth and
proof of a real person.

**Treatment.** Real photographs, unstyled. **Cadence.** One a week. **Channels.** Instagram,
occasionally LinkedIn.

## The weekly rhythm

A default to vary from, not a cage. Series rotate so no two consecutive days share a mode.

| Day | LinkedIn | X | Instagram |
|---|---|---|---|
| Mon | The Position | One Thing | The Position |
| Tue | Inside the Account | One Thing | Inside the Account |
| Wed | The Method | Method thread | The Method, carousel |
| Thu | 7am | 7am | 7am |
| Fri | The Teardown | One Thing | The Teardown, carousel |
| Sat | Off Duty | One Thing | The Mission |
| Sun | The Position | One Thing | Off Duty |

**When there is no 7am number**, Thursday takes an extra Inside the Account or Teardown. It
never takes an invented figure.

## Video, and why it is now in the mix

The reference material Josh flagged is video, and the strongest of it is barely produced: a
phone pointed at a screen while a finger moves down a column. That is inside the reach of
this system today.

**Two video formats to build first.**

**The screen sequence.** Four to six frames filmed off one continuous take, one line of text
each, each advancing the argument. Works as a Reel, a Story sequence and a carousel from the
same footage.

**The napkin.** A mechanism drawn by hand and photographed or filmed. Low fidelity is the
point — it cannot be faked and it reads as thinking rather than marketing.

Reels are produced through Higgsfield, hosted under `social/` on the site, then published.
See the skill for the pipeline.

## The treatments, which are what hold it together

| Treatment | Used by | What it is |
|---|---|---|
| **Volt mark-up** | Teardown, One Thing | B&W photo, precise volt annotation applied in code |
| **Volt redaction** | 7am, Inside the Account | Filmed screen, volt bars over every figure |
| **Design-led card** | The Method | Ink card, volt figure, full bleed |
| **Documentary B&W** | The Mission | High contrast monochrome, no volt |
| **Aspirational photograph** | The Position | Scale and standard, real places |
| **Real photograph** | Off Duty | Unstyled, phone, ordinary |

**Monochrome plus volt, everywhere except The Mission**, which drops volt so the two threads
read apart. That single constraint is what makes eight series look like one account.

---

## What this does not change

The approval gate. The claim rules. No invented number, ever, and no client identifiable.
The reference posts that inspired the Inside the Account format publish live ROAS and spend
freely. **Sevenam cannot and the redaction is not a compromise, it is the format.**
