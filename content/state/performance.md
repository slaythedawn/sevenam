# Performance

Index = the post's engagement metric divided by the trailing median for that format on
that channel across the last 12 posts. Below 12 posts on a format, leave index blank
rather than computing one off a thin median.

Only 1.5+ and 0.6- mean anything. Everything between is noise.

LinkedIn metrics are pulled by hand on Mondays. X metrics come from
`blotato_get_post_analytics`.
