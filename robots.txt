# https://sevenam.com.au

# A crawler that matches a named group below uses ONLY that group and ignores
# the wildcard one entirely — that is the robots.txt spec, not a quirk. The
# named AI groups used to carry a bare "Allow: /", which meant GPTBot,
# ClaudeBot and PerplexityBot were the three crawlers on the internet permitted
# to fetch /onboard, the private per-client intake. Every group therefore
# repeats the same four Disallow lines. Add a group and copy them, or that
# crawler gets the client intake.

User-agent: *
Allow: /
Disallow: /*.json$
Disallow: /support.js
Disallow: /image-slot.js
Disallow: /onboard

# AI crawlers: everything else here is published deliberately, so they get the
# same access as anyone. GPTBot is OpenAI's training crawler and OAI-SearchBot
# is the one behind ChatGPT search results — they are separate agents and
# allowing one does not allow the other. Google-Extended covers Gemini and
# Vertex grounding; ordinary Google Search and AI Overviews follow Googlebot
# and are already covered by the wildcard group above.
