What advertising in Sydney actually costs you.

Sydney has both the highest media costs and the highest concentration of percentage-based agency fees in Australia, which is an expensive combination: your fee rises with the same budget inflation that's already squeezing your margin.

It's also the market where in-platform ROAS gets quoted most confidently in board meetings. Blended revenue against total spend is a far harder number to argue with, and it's the one the daily decisions is built around.

Media is set by the auction, not your postcode — what you control is the fee on top.
A percentage fee rises every time you scale, for work the platform now automates.
Creative volume is the constraint in every market we've worked in, including this one.
Decision speed is the other half: most accounts wait a week to act on what the decisions shows daily.

Questions people actually ask.

Are you a Facebook ads agency in Sydney?

We're not an agency at all, and we're based in Sydney. We install the system on your own account and the decisions land at 7am in your timezone; we work with businesses across NSW remotely.

Our agency charges 12% — is that normal in Sydney?

It's common. Whether it's reasonable is a different question: put your spend into the fee calculator and look at the annual figure, then at what it becomes when you double your budget.

Do we need someone in-house?

For the self-run version, yes — about twenty minutes a day. If nobody can do that, we'll quote the end-to-end option and do the execution ourselves.
