Why the numbers aren't on this page.

Not to make you chase us. A published figure would be either too high for a brand at $30k a month or too low for one running four accounts — and the wrong number costs you more than no number.

So we do the opposite of a proposal process: one fifteen-minute call where every figure is stated plainly, in writing, including the ones that tell you not to buy. No sequence, no scarcity, no discount if you sign this week.

The rules, whatever you buy
Never a percentage of your ad spend.
Never a lock-in term or a notice period.
Never our ad account, our pixel or our creative library.
Never a discount for committing longer.
Never a change to your fee because your spend went up.

Want to see what a percentage costs over a year before you talk to anyone? Run it through the calculator.

What you're actually buying.

A month's creative, on time

Not one hero video a quarter. Distinct concepts, hooks, cuts and statics, produced on an AI-assisted line so volume isn't priced by the hour — and shipped weekly so learning compounds.

A daily decision you approve

The account is read first thing each morning and the day's changes are drafted with the numbers behind them. At 7am you tap approve and we make those changes in Ads Manager. About a minute of your day.

A fee that doesn't grow with you

Double your budget and the invoice is identical, because it was never a share of your media. The system gets more valuable as you scale, not more expensive.

Questions people actually ask.

Why aren't your prices on the website?

Because the honest figure depends on what your account needs. We publish every number on the call, in full, including what we would charge and what we would not sell you — and you leave with it in writing whether you buy or not.

Do you charge a percentage of ad spend?

Never. Every fee is fixed. Doubling your Meta budget does not change what you pay us.

Is there a lock-in contract?

No. Setup is a fixed-scope project. Creative can be bought as a one-off batch or as a monthly subscription you stop at the end of any month, with no notice period.

What happens on the consultation call?

Fifteen minutes. We look at your spend, your creative pipeline and who runs the account, then tell you which option fits and what it costs. If none of it fits, we say that.
