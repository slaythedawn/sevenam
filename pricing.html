What it costs.

The setup is four weeks of fixed scope, so it is one number rather than a range: $19,500, and it does not move afterwards. The daily decisions are $2,500 a month once it is live. How much creative an account consumes genuinely differs between a brand at $30k a month and one running four accounts, so packages start at $5,000 and we size the rest after reading the account.

The rest works the opposite way to a proposal process: one short conversation where every remaining figure is stated plainly, in writing, including the ones that tell you not to buy. No sequence, no scarcity, no discount if you sign this week.

What you're actually buying.

A month's creative, on time

Not one hero video a quarter. Distinct concepts, hooks, cuts and statics, produced end to end on an AI line so volume isn't priced by the hour — and turned around in days, so learning compounds instead of waiting on a shoot.

A daily decision you approve

The account is read first thing each morning and the day's changes are drafted with the numbers behind them. At 7am you work through them with the system and we make the changes in Ads Manager. About a minute of your day.

A fee that doesn't grow with you

Double your budget and the invoice is identical. It is priced to the work. The system gets more valuable as you scale, not more expensive.

Questions people actually ask.

Is the price fixed before we start?

Yes. The setup is $19,500 and the daily decisions are $2,500 a month, and neither moves once agreed. Creative depends on how much the account consumes, so packages start at $5,000 and we put the rest in writing once we have read it — including what we would not sell you.

Do you charge a percentage of ad spend?

Our fees are priced to the work — installing the system, running the decisions, producing the creative. None of that gets harder because you spent more, so none of it costs more.

Is there a lock-in contract?

No. Setup is a fixed-scope project. Creative is ordered when you want it, priced per concept, with no minimum and no subscription. Anything monthly stops at the end of any month with no notice period.

What happens on the consultation call?

Fifteen minutes. We look at your spend, your creative pipeline and who runs the account, then tell you which option fits and what it costs. If none of it fits, we say that.
