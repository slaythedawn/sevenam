ABOUT

A growth systems company, not an agency.

We install Meta advertising systems on businesses' own ad accounts, then run the technology that operates them.

Based in Sydney, working across Australia. No retainers, no lock-in, and no fee that climbs with your media budget.

Who builds this

I've spent a decade inside ecommerce accounts, not next to them.

I'm Josh. I've run and rebuilt Meta advertising for Australian ecommerce brands through every version of this platform — manual audiences, iOS 14, the collapse of targeting as a craft, and now delivery models that decide more than any operator does.

I built Sevenam because I kept watching good brands pay a percentage of a budget they'd scaled themselves, for work the platform had already automated. The decisions that arrives at 7am is the thing I was doing manually for clients every morning anyway — written down, made repeatable, and handed to the person who actually owns the account.

Everything I know is published: the guides, the glossary, the prices, the margins. If you can run the system yourself from what's on this site, do that — the Playbook exists for exactly that reason.

Before Sevenam

I have run the accounts I am asking you to hand over.

Fifteen years across ecommerce, consumer goods and the agencies that serve them — as an operator carrying a P&L, not a consultant presenting to one. The short version:

Turnarounds

Napoleon Perdis: took over a declining premium cosmetics brand with 400+ SKUs across 80 global suppliers and three years of excess stock. Repositioned it, rebuilt the supply chain and tripled ecommerce.

New markets

Took SRW and Eimele into the United States from scratch — 3PL, local sites, Amazon, regulatory compliance, and the paid social and PR that filled them.

Building brands

Simple Complex and Theronomic from concept to market: formula, positioning, packaging and go-to-market. Rebranded six others, including SRW and Eimele.

Scaling operations

Head of Marketing and Growth at VOLY, the Sequoia-backed 15-minute grocery service, through the scale from six people to two hundred in under six months.

Running a business

Founded and ran the Seoul office for Iris, full P&L, profitable inside twelve months. Later Managing Partner at VML with shared P&L for a thirty-million-dollar unit.

Category depth

Three years across longevity, supplements, beauty and plant-based nutrition at Access Corporate — ten owned brands, fifty-six markets — including the clinical studies behind the claims.

Agency side

Campaigns for Coca-Cola, Samsung, Diageo, McDonald's, Netflix, Nivea and Red Bull. Ran Australia's largest social community for McDonald's for three years; Cannes Lions and Effie wins for the work.

Hands on the tools

Shopify, Klaviyo, Amazon, TikTok Shop and the app ecosystem around them. I can still build and run the stack myself, which is why the system is built to be handed over.
