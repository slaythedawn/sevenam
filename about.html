ABOUT

A growth systems company, not an agency.

We build growth systems with AI and technology — Meta advertising installed on your own ad account, the creative that feeds it, and the marketing automation underneath the wider business.

Based in Sydney, working with ecommerce and founder-led businesses anywhere the auction runs. No retainers, no lock-in, and fees priced to the work.

Who builds this

Work with a founder who has built and scaled their own brands, not an agency.

I'm Josh. I've run and rebuilt Meta advertising for Australian ecommerce brands through every version of this platform — manual audiences, iOS 14, the collapse of targeting as a craft, and now delivery models that decide more than any operator does.

I built Sevenam because I kept watching good brands pay a percentage of a budget they'd scaled themselves, for work the platform had already automated. The decision that arrives at 7am is the thing I was doing manually for clients every morning anyway — written down, made repeatable, and handed to the person who actually owns the account.

Everything I know is published: the guides, the glossary, the benchmarks, the prices, the margins. If you can run the system yourself from what's on this site, do that — the Playbook exists for exactly that reason.
