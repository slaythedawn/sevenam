ABOUT

A growth systems company, not an agency.

We install Meta advertising systems on businesses' own ad accounts, then run the technology that operates them.

Based in Sydney, working across Australia. No retainers, no lock-in, and no fee that climbs with your media budget.

Who builds this

I've spent a decade inside ecommerce accounts, not next to them.

I'm Josh. I've run and rebuilt Meta advertising for Australian ecommerce brands through every version of this platform — manual audiences, iOS 14, the collapse of targeting as a craft, and now delivery models that decide more than any operator does.

I built Sevenam because I kept watching good brands pay a percentage of a budget they'd scaled themselves, for work the platform had already automated. The decisions that arrives at 7am is the thing I was doing manually for clients every morning anyway — written down, made repeatable, and handed to the person who actually owns the account.

Everything I know is published: the guides, the glossary, the prices, the margins. If you can run the system yourself from what's on this site, do that — the Playbook exists for exactly that reason.
